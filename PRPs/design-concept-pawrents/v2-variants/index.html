Pawrent v2 · Concept Variants

Three directional explorations against the v2 brief palette (burgundy · sage · POPs tri-color) and Garet typography. Each variant covers the same 5 locked-nav screens for side-by-side comparison. Static HTML mockups — open at 390×844 (iPhone 14) in DevTools for best fit.

VARIANT A
Editorial Pet Journal
Sage surfaces, burgundy authority type, Fraunces serif mastheads. POPs tri-color reserved for celebratory moments only. Feels like a thoughtfully curated magazine.

VARIANT B
Sticker Studio
POPs pink/coral/yellow as bold color blocks, chunky 24–32px radii with 2px black outlines, sticker-style UI and mascot emojis. Y2K pet culture, share-first.
หน้าหลัก สัตว์เลี้ยง ฟีด แจ้งเตือน โปรไฟล์
VARIANT C
Soft Dossier
Tight 12-col grid, burgundy tabular numerals, micro-charts and completion rings, § section marks. Pet passport as portfolio — data-forward without clinical coldness.
